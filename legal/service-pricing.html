Lagna Setu delivers exclusively purely digital, software-based matrimonial services. There are no physical goods, parcels, or couriers associated with this platform.

2. Mode of Delivery: Access is unlocked automatically inside your active web session. No physical delivery is required. You will also receive an automated transaction confirmation directly on your registered email address.

3. Electronic Invoice / Payment Proof: A formal digital payment receipt showing the Razorpay Payment ID, date, time, and ₹49 amount is available immediately upon checkout and stored in your account history.

3 100% UPI Mode Payment Assurance

In compliance with the highest standards of Indian digital payments security:

  • Strictly UPI Only: We accept payments exclusively via official Unified Payments Interface (UPI) apps—including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred, and bank UPI VPAs.
  • Cards & Net Banking Disabled: Credit cards, debit cards, net banking, and wallets are disabled to ensure friction-free and safe mobile transactions.
  • Encrypted Gateway: All payment sessions are handled through Razorpay's PCI-DSS Level 1 certified infrastructure.
