Verification & safety

No fake profiles. That's a rule, not a promise.

Matrimony only works when the person on the other side of the profile is real. So we built the whole platform around one non-negotiable step: proof, checked by a human, before anyone goes live.

A verification document is mandatory

Every member uploads a School Leaving Certificate (LC) or a Caste Certificate during registration — no exceptions.

Manually reviewed, not auto-approved

Our admin team checks the document against the profile — name, community and details — before approval is granted.

Hidden until verified

Profiles stay out of search results entirely while verification is pending — nobody meets an unverified account.

Unclear document? It's rejected, with a reason

If a document is blurry or doesn't match, we reject it and explain why — the member can then re-upload and try again.

Reportable, always

Every profile carries a "Report" option, so if something looks wrong after approval, our team can step back in immediately.
